About BRT

Where research meets implementation

Behavioral Research and Teaching is a University of Oregon research unit focused on improving educational assessment and outcomes.

Our work includes assessment development, technical documentation, reliability and classification research, alignment studies, alternate assessment, and tools that help educators use student data.
