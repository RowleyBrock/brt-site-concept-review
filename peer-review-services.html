Participation, Accessibility & Implementation

Ways to work with BRT

Choose the level of support the decision requires

Preliminary planning ranges help agencies match a technical need to an appropriate project scale. Final scope and pricing are established through a University of Oregon proposal.

01 / FOCUSED GUIDANCE

Advice & Resources

$5,000 to $20,000

For a bounded question that benefits from independent university-based expertise.

States may receive

  • Two to four working sessions
  • Focused evidence or literature scan
  • Decision memo or annotated resource set
  • Review of a draft plan or technical approach

Preliminary project planning range.

02 / DEFINED PRIORITY

Targeted Consultation

$20,000 to $100,000

For a peer-review response, independent review, or technical priority with defined boundaries.

States may receive

  • Federal peer-review gap analysis
  • TAC briefing and consultation
  • Independent review findings
  • Evidence map and improvement roadmap

Preliminary project planning range.

03 / DESIGNED STUDY

Technical Studies & Implementation

$75,000 to $300,000+

For new evidence, complex analyses, technical documentation, or multi-phase implementation work.

States may receive

  • Alignment or psychometric study
  • Reliability, validity, or growth analyses
  • Technical report and documentation
  • Implementation study and recommendations

Preliminary project planning range.
