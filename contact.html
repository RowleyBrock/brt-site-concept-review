What happens next

From inquiry to an actionable scope

  1. BRT reviews the context.
    We identify the service area, initial questions, and whether the requested work fits BRT's expertise.
  2. The parties discuss scope.
    State or partner representatives and BRT clarify methods, deliverables, roles, timing, and constraints.
  3. BRT develops a University of Oregon proposal.
    The proposal documents the proposed scope, schedule, project team structure, and budget.
  4. The agreement is executed and work begins.
    The appropriate state agency contract or assessment vendor partnership agreement establishes the project.
