Technical service 02

Standards, Alignment & Assessment Design

Build defensible connections from intended content to blueprints, items, and score interpretations.

Discuss this serviceReview deliverables

A realistic state challenge

Standards or blueprints have changed, but the state lacks current evidence that assessment content adequately represents the intended expectations.

The agency may need an independent alignment study, a clearer alternate assessment framework, or technical support responding to design-related peer-review findings.

What BRT would investigate

  • Content representation across standards, frameworks, blueprints, and items
  • Depth, breadth, and balance of assessed knowledge and skills
  • Linkage between alternate assessment content and grade-level expectations
  • Documentation supporting design decisions and intended interpretations

Representative methods

Make design evidence systematic and reviewable

  • Alignment study design
  • Expert panel protocols
  • Blueprint and item mapping
  • Content coverage analysis
  • Framework review
  • Documentation synthesis

Specific deliverables

Alignment study package

Protocol, reviewer training, rating materials, analysis, findings, and limitations.

Design review

Independent findings on content representation, blueprint balance, and documented rationale.

Technical narrative

Clear evidence explaining how standards, design decisions, and intended score meaning connect.

Relevant BRT research

ORExt linkage, alignment, and frameworks

The 2025 Oregon Extended Assessment Linkage and Alignment Study evaluated updated standards, alternate academic achievement standards, complexity parameters, field-test items, and accessibility across ELA, mathematics, and science. ORExt Essentialized Assessment Frameworks provide related design evidence.
