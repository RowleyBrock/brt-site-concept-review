Technical service 03

Psychometrics, Validity & Reliability

Develop evidence that scores are sufficiently consistent, meaningful, and appropriate for their intended uses.

Discuss this serviceReview deliverables

A realistic state challenge

The technical manual reports routine statistics, but evidence is incomplete for a high-stakes interpretation, classification decision, or peer-review criterion.

The state needs an independent study plan that connects statistical analyses to the claims and decisions the assessment is expected to support.

What BRT would investigate

  • Score consistency and classification accuracy
  • Validity evidence supporting intended score uses
  • Scaling, linking, comparability, and standard-setting evidence
  • Subgroup results, limitations, and priorities for future study

Representative methods

Connect analyses to the decisions they support

Advanced model evaluation compares plausible psychometric models, checks assumptions, and documents whether conclusions remain stable across specifications.

  • Reliability estimation
  • Classification consistency analysis
  • Validity argument mapping
  • Unidimensional and multidimensional IRT model comparison
  • Item fit, person fit, and local-dependence diagnostics
  • Scaling, linking, and equating sensitivity analyses
  • Standard-setting consultation
  • Reproducible code and independent result checks

Specific deliverables

Analysis plan

Research questions, data requirements, methods, decision rules, and reporting specifications.

Technical study report

Reproducible analyses, results, limitations, interpretations, and recommendations.

Validity evidence roadmap

Claims, available evidence, gaps, priority studies, and documentation plan.
