Psychometrics specialty

Measurement Fairness & DIF Analysis

Evaluate whether items and scores function comparably across relevant student groups and document evidence for review and action.

Discuss a fairness studyReview deliverables

State assessment problem

Routine subgroup summaries do not explain whether observed differences reflect item behavior, construct representation, opportunity to learn, or other factors.

BRT can combine statistical screening with content review and validity evidence. DIF flags prompt technical and substantive review; they do not by themselves establish item bias.

What BRT would investigate

  • Uniform and nonuniform differential item functioning
  • Effect size, statistical power, sample-size limitations, and multiplicity
  • Score comparability and classification patterns across groups
  • Interactions among accessibility features, item content, and subgroup performance
  • Consistency of findings across forms, years, grades, and methods

Representative methods

Combine statistical and content evidence

  • IRT likelihood-ratio DIF analysis
  • Logistic-regression DIF analysis
  • Multigroup confirmatory factor analysis
  • Effect-size and sensitivity analysis
  • Subgroup reliability and classification review
  • Structured item-content review

What a state can receive

Fairness analysis plan

Priority groups, sample requirements, methods, effect-size rules, review workflow, and documentation standards.

DIF and subgroup findings

Item-level results, method comparisons, uncertainty, recurring patterns, and prioritized review sets.

Review-panel package

Reviewer training, item evidence summaries, structured judgments, disposition records, and technical-report language.

Research foundation

Fairness, accessibility, and DIF evidence

Park, Ahn, and Zopluoglu’s 2021 simulation study examines DIF effect size using multigroup confirmatory factor analysis. The 2026 ORExt Technical Documentation connects fairness analyses with accessibility, measurement precision, classification quality, and score interpretation.
