Automated Scoring Evaluation & Validation

Independently evaluate whether automated scores are accurate, fair, stable, explainable, and appropriate for their intended decisions.

Discuss a scoring evaluationReview deliverables

State assessment problem

An automated scoring system performs well overall, but the agency needs evidence about errors, subgroups, items, and operational conditions.

BRT can design an independent validation that connects scoring performance to intended uses, human-score quality, construct coverage, fairness, and monitoring requirements.

What BRT would investigate

  • Agreement with qualified human scoring and adjudication outcomes
  • Error rates by item, score point, response type, and subgroup
  • Construct-relevant and construct-irrelevant score behavior
  • Training, validation, holdout, and challenge-set design
  • Drift, version changes, exception routing, and human review

Representative methods

Evaluate scores, errors, fairness, and operations together

  • Human-machine agreement analysis
  • Quadratic weighted kappa and confusion analysis
  • Residual and score-point error analysis
  • Subgroup and differential performance review
  • Robustness and challenge-set evaluation
  • Version and drift monitoring design

What a state can receive

Validation design

Claims, samples, human-score requirements, performance criteria, subgroup analyses, and decision rules.

Independent evaluation report

Accuracy, agreement, error patterns, fairness evidence, limitations, and recommendations for operational use.

Monitoring and governance plan

Drift indicators, resampling cadence, version controls, exception routing, and triggers for human review or retraining.

Applied automated-scoring research

BRT Technical Report 2301 by Cengiz Zopluoglu and Gerald Tindal explores large language models for automated essay scoring in WriteRightNow. Zopluoglu also led the University of Oregon’s runner-up entry in the 2023 NAEP Math Automated Scoring Challenge, which evaluated accuracy and bias evidence.
