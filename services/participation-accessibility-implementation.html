Technical service 01

Participation, Accessibility & Implementation

Turn statewide policy into consistent, accessible practice and credible implementation evidence.

Discuss this serviceReview deliverables

A realistic state challenge

Participation decisions vary across districts, and the state cannot show that guidance and training are working as intended.

The agency may also need stronger evidence for federal peer review, clearer monitoring indicators, or a defensible plan for evaluating accommodations and alternate assessment participation.

What BRT would investigate

  • Alignment among policy, guidance, training, and local decision making
  • Patterns in participation, accommodations, and exception data
  • Educator understanding and implementation barriers
  • Strength and completeness of monitoring and peer-review evidence

Representative methods

Study policy and practice together

  • Document and evidence review
  • Policy-to-practice mapping
  • Survey and interview design
  • Participation and subgroup analysis
  • Training content review
  • Implementation indicator development

Specific deliverables

Participation evidence review

Findings organized by policy, decision rules, data, training, monitoring, and evidence gaps.

Implementation study

Study protocol, instruments, analysis, findings, limitations, and actionable recommendations.

Improvement roadmap

Prioritized actions, evidence owners, monitoring indicators, and documentation needs.

Relevant BRT research

Alternate assessment participation and accountability

BRT Technical Report 2607 reviews state accountability programs using state repositories, federal reviews, and national sources. It addresses participation and inclusion, the 1% cap, accessible assessment, accommodations, technical analyses, and reporting. The 2026 ORExt technical documentation provides complementary evidence on fairness, accessibility, administration, and implementation.
